# Clover Hill

A calm 3D farm stroll. Wander a pasture at golden hour and say hello to the cows, sheep, horses, pigs and hens.

Single `index.html`, Three.js loaded from a CDN, no build step. Live at https://erictempleton1.github.io/clover-hill/ — Pages serves the `gh-pages` branch, which `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` mirrors from `main` on every push.

**Controls:** WASD to walk, Shift to run, drag to look, scroll to zoom, E to say hello. On touch, drag on the left half of the screen to walk.
